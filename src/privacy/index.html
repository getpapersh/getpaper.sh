PaperMac Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

PaperMac has no accounts, ads or usage analytics. Licensing and update checks make their own network requests, listed below.

What leaves your Mac, and why

When What is sent To
You buy Pro The name, email, payment and billing details you enter at checkout. We see your order and the billing details Dodo Payments shares with us, such as your name, email, phone number and billing address, and your card's network, type, issuing country, last four digits and cardholder name. Dodo Payments, the merchant of record (privacy policy)
You activate, check or deactivate a license Your license key and your IP address. Activating also sends your Mac's name, which often includes the owner's name; checking and deactivating send the activation ID Dodo Payments gave this Mac instead. The app rechecks the license from time to time. Dodo Payments, through its public license service
The app checks for updates (usually daily, or when you ask) Your IP address and the PaperMac and Sparkle versions Our download server, dl.getpaper.sh
You email us Whatever you send, including any diagnostics file you choose to attach Our email provider, Fastmail

The licensing and update requests never include window titles, app or file names, documents or anything else on screen.

A diagnostics file is different. PaperMac saves it only where you choose and never uploads it, but it contains your logs, settings, window titles, app names and file paths that include your account name. If you attach it to an email, we receive all of that.

What stays on your Mac

  • Desktop names and summaries. Apple's on-device model creates them. Your window contents aren't sent anywhere.
  • Your license record. PaperMac keeps its copy in your Mac's Keychain. Dodo Payments also holds license and activation records.
  • Logs and diagnostics. They stay on your Mac unless you export them and send them to us.

The website

getpaper.sh has no analytics, ads or third-party scripts, and sets no cookies of its own. Cloudflare delivers the site and receives standard request data, such as your IP address and the page you asked for, so it can serve pages and protect the site.

An activation link that contains your license key sends the key to Cloudflare as part of the address when you open it. The page then removes the key from the address bar and doesn't store it. Treat that link like a password.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or build profiles of you. We don't track you across sites, so Do Not Track signals change nothing.

Keeping and deleting data

Dodo Payments keeps purchase, license and activation records under its own privacy policy, and Cloudflare keeps website and update-server request data under its own. We keep support email for as long as we need it to help you.

Email support@getpaper.sh to get a copy of your data, correct, delete or move it, limit or object to how we use it, or complain about how we handle it. Where the law gives you more privacy rights, including in California, the EU and the UK, you have them here too.

EU and UK

We use your data on these grounds:

  • Contract: to sell and license PaperMac and support your purchase.
  • Legal obligation: to handle your privacy requests.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep tax and accounting records, deliver updates, answer general support email and keep the website secure.

We're based in the US. Dodo Payments, Cloudflare and Fastmail process data in the US and other countries. Email us with questions about how your data is transferred. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

Other

  • Children. PaperMac isn't directed to children under 13.
  • Changes. If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects what is sent from your Mac, we'll also say so in the app's release notes.
  • Ownership changes. If PaperMac moves to a company we control or is sold, your data moves with it and stays under this policy.

Who "we" are: PaperMac's developer, Jason Martin.
