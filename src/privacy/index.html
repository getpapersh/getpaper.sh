PaperMac Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

PaperMac has no accounts or ads. Usage sharing is off unless you turn it on. Licensing and update checks make their own network requests, listed below.

What leaves your Mac, and why

When What is sent To
You buy Pro The name, email, payment and billing details you enter at checkout. We see your name, email, country and order. Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record (privacy policy)
You activate, check or deactivate a license Your license key, your IP address, and either your Mac's name or an activation ID. Mac names often include the owner's name. The app rechecks the license from time to time, usually during update checks. Lemon Squeezy
The app checks for updates (usually daily, or when you ask) Your IP address and the PaperMac and Sparkle versions Our download server, dl.getpaper.sh
You turn on usage sharing (off by default) Below this table. PostHog, hosted in the EU (privacy policy), and our download server
You email us Whatever you send, including any diagnostics file you choose to attach Our email provider, Fastmail

When usage sharing is on, PostHog receives:

  • a random ID created when you opt in;
  • which features you use each day;
  • onboarding steps, trial and activation events, and the Pro limits you hit;
  • error codes;
  • your PaperMac and macOS versions;
  • whether you use the free, trial or Pro version.

About once a week, update checks also send our download server a basic system profile: Mac model, processor, memory, macOS version and preferred language.

Usage sharing never includes window titles, app or file names, documents or anything else on screen. PostHog and our download server both receive your IP address.

You can turn sharing off in Settings at any time. That stops new data and deletes the ID on your Mac. It doesn't delete data already sent.

What stays on your Mac

  • Desktop names and summaries. Apple's on-device model creates them. Your window contents aren't sent anywhere.
  • Your license record. PaperMac keeps its copy in your Mac's Keychain. Lemon Squeezy also holds license and activation records.
  • Logs and diagnostics. They stay on your Mac unless you export them and send them to us.

The website

getpaper.sh has no analytics, ads or third-party scripts, and sets no cookies of its own. Cloudflare delivers the site and receives standard request data, such as your IP address and the page you asked for, so it can serve pages and protect the site.

The activation link in your receipt contains your license key, so Cloudflare receives the key as part of the address when you open the link. The page then removes the key from the address bar and doesn't store it. Treat that link like a password.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or build profiles of you. We don't track you across sites, so Do Not Track signals change nothing.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep:

  • purchase records for as long as tax and accounting law requires;
  • license and activation records for as long as your license is valid;
  • support email for as long as we need it to help you;
  • usage data only while it helps us improve PaperMac, then we delete it (we may keep totals that can't identify anyone);
  • website and update-server request data only as long as Cloudflare needs it to serve and protect them; we keep update-check totals that can't identify anyone.

Email support@getpaper.sh to get a copy of your data, correct, delete or move it, limit or object to how we use it, or complain about how we handle it. Usage data is tied only to a random ID that isn't linked to your license or email, so we usually can't find it for a request. Where the law gives you more privacy rights, including in California, the EU and the UK, you have them here too.

EU and UK

We use your data on these grounds:

  • Contract: to sell and license PaperMac and support your purchase.
  • Legal obligation: to handle your privacy requests.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep tax and accounting records, deliver updates, answer general support email and keep the website secure.
  • Consent: for usage sharing only. You can withdraw it in Settings at any time.

We're based in the US. Lemon Squeezy, Cloudflare and Fastmail process data in the US and other countries; PostHog processes usage data in the EU. Transfers out of the EU and UK rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, and their UK equivalents. Email us for a copy of those safeguards. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

Other

  • Children. PaperMac isn't directed to children under 13.
  • Changes. If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects what is sent from your Mac, we'll also say so in the app's release notes.
  • Ownership changes. If PaperMac moves to a company we control or is sold, your data moves with it and stays under this policy.

Who "we" are: PaperMac's developer, Jason Martin.
