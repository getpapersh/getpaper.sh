How is PaperMac's engine different?

PaperMac was written from scratch. On macOS 27, it runs on an engine that gives every window its own container. As you scroll, windows stay full size on their own display instead of being pushed off-screen or squeezed to fit, so nothing peeks onto your other monitor and apps don't reflow. Windows you've scrolled away from keep running in the background, ready the moment you come back.
